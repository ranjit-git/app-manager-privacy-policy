# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **App Manager: Analyze & Backup** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information remains secure. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles data on your device.

Because our core philosophy is privacy-first, the fundamental principle of our application is simple: **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data or usage statistics.**

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
**App Manager operates entirely locally on your device.** 

We do **not** use external servers, analytics SDKs, or tracking frameworks to monitor your behavior. Any data generated, analyzed, or displayed by the app (including app lists, screen time, storage metrics, and backups) remains strictly on your device.

## 3. Required Permissions and Why We Need Them
To provide its core functionality, App Manager requires certain Android permissions. These permissions are used exclusively to provide features within the app and are never used to harvest data.

*   **`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` (Query All Packages):**
    *   *Purpose:* Required to list all user and system applications installed on your device so you can manage, back up, and analyze them.
*   **`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS` (Usage Access):**
    *   *Purpose:* Required for the "Insights" dashboard to display screen time, network consumption, and storage usage metrics. This data is processed locally and never leaves your device.
*   **`REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES` (Delete Packages):**
    *   *Purpose:* Allows the app to prompt you to uninstall applications when you select the "Uninstall" or "Batch Uninstall" features.
*   **Storage Access (Read/Write External Storage):**
    *   *Purpose:* Necessary to extract APK files, save backup snapshots, and export reports to your device's storage.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or crash-reporting services (such as Firebase or Google Analytics) that might track your behavior across other apps and websites.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure
Because we do not collect your data, we have no data to share, sell, or disclose to third parties. The only way data leaves the app is if you manually choose to export and share it (e.g., sharing an extracted APK or backup snapshot via the Android Share Sheet).

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from anyone under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect user data, it is inherently compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## 7. GDPR and CCPA Compliance
Because App Manager does not collect, process, or store personal data off-device, it complies fully with the data minimization and privacy-by-design principles outlined in the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). 

*   **Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten:** You have full control over your data. You can delete any backups or extracted APKs directly through the app or your device's file manager. Uninstalling the app removes all associated local configurations.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if we introduce new features that require different device permissions. If any changes occur, we will update this document and the "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
